 Stemming:
Definition: Stemming chops off the end of the word to find its root form, often resulting in non-dictionary words.

Method: Rule-based and fast.

Example:

"running" → "run"

"flies" → "fli"

"studies" → "studi"

Disadvantage: Can produce words that are not valid English words


 Lemmatization:
Definition: Lemmatization reduces a word to its dictionary form (lemma), considering the context and part of speech.

Method: Uses vocabulary and morphological analysis.

Example:

"running" → "run"

"flies" → "fly"

"studies" → "study"

Advantage: More accurate than stemming, but slower.
